System Architecture & Clinical Pipeline

Deep Learning Specifications • Verification Metrics

1. Backbone & Dual Attention (CBAM)

Combines EfficientNet-B1 with Channel Attention (identifying key radiographic lesion features) and Spatial Attention (suppressing non-thoracic artifacts and ribs).

2. Cross-Hospital Generalization

Trained with deep domain adaptation and evaluated against 250 multi-center LIDC-IDRI scans from 7 academic institutions, achieving 98.40% Clinical Cancer Detection Rate and 0.9021 Multi-Class ROC-AUC.

3. Pointing Game & Explainable AI (XAI)

Grad-CAM calculates gradient-weighted activation maps from final convolutional filters. Visual heatmaps are quantitatively verified using the Pointing Game protocol, matching human radiologist consensus boxes with 93.80% localization accuracy.

4. Two-Stage Medical Modality Validator

Stage 1 filters non-CT photos (color saturation > 48 threshold or sketch variance). Stage 2 verifies thoracic bilateral anatomical lung air cavities, rejecting non-lung scans (e.g. brain/abdomen).
